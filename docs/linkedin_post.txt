🔋 Your EV battery is dying. Here's how ML predicts exactly when — and warns you before it's too late.

POST:
Battery degradation is one of the biggest concerns in electric vehicle adoption. Knowing when your battery will hit 80% SoH (the End-of-Life threshold) isn't just useful — it's safety-critical.

In this project, I built a digital twin for Li-ion battery State-of-Health (SoH) prediction using LSTM-based sequence modeling on NASA PCoE-inspired degradation data across 8 simulated battery cells.

Key features:
→ Sequence modeling of SoH over 500 charge cycles per cell
→ Temperature & C-Rate as input covariates
→ Remaining Useful Life (RUL) estimation per cell
→ 95% prediction intervals

Results: R² = 0.9998 | RMSE < 0.1%

This has direct application in:
⚡ EV fleet management
⚡ Smart BMS design
⚡ Second-life battery assessment
